## Canonical frame

Clay statement (B) permits periodic data with nonzero mean. For zero forcing the spatial mean `m` is conserved, and the exact Galilean transform

```text
v(x,t) = u(x + m t,t) - m
```

reduces the problem to a zero-mean periodic field without changing global regularity. Because speed-based diagnostics are not Galilean invariant, all amplitude/iso-speed quantities in the current proof spine are frozen in this canonical zero-mean frame. R21/C001 record the theorem and a nonzero-mean travelling-shear falsifier showing why this frame discipline matters.

### 3. Bernoulli-gradient / pressure branch

R03 gives

```text

```

R21 strengthens the iso-speed analysis: in the canonical zero-mean frame, **each connected regular iso-speed component has zero net velocity flux separately**, removing R10's previously retained inter-component pressure-offset × flux obstruction.

R22 then eliminates level-set topology from the identity itself. With the periodic vector potential `A=curl^{-1}u` and `rho=|u|`,

```text
u·∇rho = div(A×∇rho),
Q(rho u) = Q(A×∇rho),
W_3 = <Q(ω×u), Q(A×∇rho)>.
```

RD012 proves the Helmholtz projection is load-bearing: a smooth high-frequency family has `||A×∇rho||_2` growing like frequency while its projected part remains uniformly bounded.

R23 identifies the exact harmonic-analysis cancellation:


For one Fourier interaction `p+q=k`, `p·u_hat(p)=0`,


Thus high-velocity / low-amplitude-frequency interactions gain a low/high factor. The opposite low-velocity/high-amplitude and high-high regimes remain potentially critical.
