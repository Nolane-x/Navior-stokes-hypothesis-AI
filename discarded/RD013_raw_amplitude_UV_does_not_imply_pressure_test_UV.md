# RD013 — Raw amplitude ultraviolet does not imply pressure-test ultraviolet

**Status:** `exact no-go / representation hazard`  
**Kills:** any route that treats high Fourier frequency of `|u|` itself as evidence of high-frequency Branch-G pressure defect  
**Does not kill:** R23/R24 commutator or transported-speed routes

R23 separates the product `Q(|u|u)` into velocity/amplitude frequency interactions. A tempting next move is to argue that a high-frequency amplitude block `P_j|u|` must reflect genuinely high-frequency velocity input and therefore can be controlled through velocity/vorticity UV criteria.

That inference is false even for a smooth, nowhere-zero, exactly low-frequency velocity field.

## 1. Exact smooth transverse shear

On the periodic torus, take

> `u(x,y,z) = (2 cos z, sin z, 0)`.

Then:

- `u` is smooth;
- `div u=0`;
- `mean(u)=0`;
- the velocity has Fourier support only at the two modes `k=(0,0,+/-1)`;
- `|u|` never vanishes.

Its speed is

`rho(z)=sqrt(4 cos^2 z + sin^2 z)`

`      =sqrt((5+3 cos 2z)/2)`.

## 2. The speed has infinitely many Fourier harmonics

`rho` is positive, smooth, nonconstant, and `pi`-periodic.

Suppose for contradiction that `rho` were a trigonometric polynomial. Because it is `pi`-periodic, only even harmonics can occur. Let `2N>=2` be its highest nonzero harmonic. Then `rho^2` has a nonzero harmonic at `4N`, generated by the square of the top Fourier coefficient.

But exactly

`rho^2=(5+3 cos 2z)/2`

has highest harmonic `2`.

This is impossible. The alternative `N=0` would make `rho` constant, also false.

Therefore:

> `rho=|u|` has infinitely many nonzero Fourier harmonics even though `u` itself is supported only at |k|=1.

This is not a cusp artifact: the speed is strictly positive and analytic.

## 3. Yet the entire Helmholtz test defect is zero

The field

`rho u=(2 rho(z) cos z, rho(z) sin z,0)`

depends only on `z` and has no `z` component. Hence

`div(rho u)=0`.

Therefore

> `Q(rho u)=0` exactly.

Equivalently, every Fourier coefficient of `rho u` is transverse to its wavevector, so the gradient Helmholtz projector kills every mode.

In R24 language,

`q_amp=u·grad rho=0`

because `grad rho` is parallel to `e_z` while `u·e_z=0`.

Thus this state has:

- arbitrarily high raw amplitude frequencies;
- zero transported-speed scalar;
- zero pressure-test defect.

## 4. General transverse-shear firewall

More generally, let

`u(x)=a f(k·x)`

with constant vectors `a,k` satisfying

`a·k=0`.

Then `u` is divergence-free, `rho=|a||f(k·x)|` depends only on `k·x`, and

`u·grad rho=0`

wherever the derivative is defined. Distributionally,

`div(rho u)=0`,

so

> `Q(rho u)=0`.

The same conclusion holds for smooth positive regularizations of the amplitude.

## 5. Consequence for the E23 frontier

RD013 falsifies the shortcut

> “high `|u|` frequency => physical pressure-test UV.”

The correct object is not `P_j|u|` by itself. It is the projected/transported combination

`Q(|u|u)=grad Delta^{-1}(u·grad|u|)`.

Accordingly, the unresolved R23 `low-u/high-rho` regime must be reformulated in terms of **longitudinal coupling** between the velocity and the amplitude gradient. Purely transverse amplitude harmonics are projection-null.

This is directly analogous in spirit to RD010: a representation can manufacture ultraviolet structure that is absent from the physical obstruction. RD010 concerned a sharp amplitude cutoff; RD013 concerns the nonlinear amplitude variable itself.
