# R09 — Iso-speed pressure-fluctuation reduction

**Status:** `exact structural theorem / not claimed novel`  
**Depends on:** R01  
**Related to:** R03, R06, R08  
**Clay status:** partial structural reduction only

R01 identifies the critical pressure work

`W_3 = ∫ p q`,

where

`q = u·grad rho`, `rho=|u|`.

R09 proves that `q` annihilates **every scalar function of velocity amplitude**. Consequently, pressure can drive critical `L^3` growth only through its variation *within* iso-speed classes, not through any component determined solely by `rho`.

## 1. Infinite-dimensional amplitude cancellation

Let `F` be any sufficiently regular one-variable function and let `H` satisfy

`H'(s)=F(s)`.

Then

`F(rho) q = F(rho) u·grad rho = u·grad H(rho)`.

On the periodic domain,

`∫ u·grad H(rho)`

`= -∫ H(rho) div u = 0`.

Therefore

> `∫ F(|u|) [u·grad|u|] dx = 0`

for every admissible amplitude function `F`.

This contains R03's observation that the kinetic term `|u|^2/2` is invisible to `W_3`, but is strictly more general: **every** amplitude-only scalar is invisible.

## 2. Conditional-expectation formulation

For a fixed smooth time slice, let `S_rho` denote the closed `L^2` subspace of scalar functions measurable with respect to the sigma-algebra generated by `rho`. Equivalently, it is the closure of amplitude-only functions `F(rho)`.

Let

`Pi_rho p = E[p | sigma(rho)]`

be the `L^2` conditional expectation / orthogonal projection of pressure onto `S_rho`.

Since `q` is orthogonal to `S_rho`, exactly

> `W_3 = < p - Pi_rho p, q >`.

Define the **iso-speed pressure fluctuation**

`p_iso_perp = p - Pi_rho p`.

Then only `p_iso_perp` contributes to critical `L^3` growth.

## 3. Coarea/level-set interpretation

At times where the standard coarea representation is applied, define for almost every regular value `s`

`J(s) = ∫_{rho=s} [u·grad rho]/|grad rho| dS`.

The amplitude cancellation for arbitrary test functions `F` implies

> `J(s)=0` for almost every regular amplitude level `s`.

Hence

`W_3`

`= ∫ ds ∫_{rho=s} p [u·grad rho]/|grad rho| dS`

is unchanged if one subtracts **any** scalar `c(s)` on each level surface:

> `W_3 = ∫ ds ∫_{rho=s} [p-c(s)]`

> `              * [u·grad rho]/|grad rho| dS`.

This makes the geometry explicit: the net streamline flux across each iso-speed level cancels. Pressure work arises only because pressure is nonuniform along that level.

The conditional-expectation statement in Section 2 is the preferred rigorous formulation because it does not require every level to be regular.

## 4. A family of pressure-oscillation bounds

For any admissible amplitude function `F`, Holder gives

`|W_3| <= ||p-F(rho)||_6 ||q||_(6/5)`.

The R06 calculation gives

`||q||_(6/5)`

`<= (1/sqrt(2)) ||u||_(3/2)^(1/2) D_3^(1/2)`

before the periodic dual-Sobolev constant is applied to pressure gradients.

Therefore

> `|W_3| <= (1/sqrt(2)) O_6(p|rho)`

> `          * ||u||_(3/2)^(1/2) D_3^(1/2)`,

where

> `O_6(p|rho) = inf_F ||p-F(rho)||_6`.

The same construction can be made in other dual exponent pairs.

This gives a new sufficient target: it is unnecessary to control the full pressure in a critical estimate if one can control only its **distance from the amplitude-generated scalar subspace**.

## 5. Relation to the Bernoulli/Lamb representation

R03 replaces `p` by the Bernoulli function

`B=p+rho^2/2`

because `rho^2/2` is amplitude-only. R09 shows that this is one member of an infinite equivalence class:

> `p ~ p + F(rho)`

with respect to the `L^3` pressure-work pairing.

Thus the truly relevant pressure variable is not `p` or `B` separately, but the quotient class of pressure modulo amplitude-only scalars.

## 6. New proof frontier

R09 suggests a geometric route that is distinct from the raw weighted-projector route killed by RD004:

1. establish Poincare/Sobolev control of pressure oscillation **along iso-speed level sets**;
2. relate tangential pressure variation to the pressure Poisson equation and the Lamb/strain geometry;
3. exploit R08: only high-amplitude levels need to support a dangerous tail;
4. prove that on those levels the pressure oscillation cannot remain large enough, for long enough, to make the critical action diverge.

The central missing quantity is therefore an iso-speed pressure-oscillation functional, not the total pressure norm.

## 7. Limitations

R09 gives no a priori bound on `O_6(p|rho)`. Iso-speed surfaces may be disconnected, highly curved, or topologically complicated, and their Poincare constants may degenerate near critical values of `rho`. Those are explicit proof obligations rather than hidden assumptions.

A successful continuation of this route must either control that geometry dynamically or find a non-geometric formulation of the quotient pressure norm.
