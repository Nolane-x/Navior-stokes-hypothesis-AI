# R33 — Exact smooth square-partition commutator synchronization

**Status:** `exact structural theorem / smooth-filter redesign`  
**Depends on:** R03–R05, R29, R32, RD015  
**Clay status:** exposes the exact smooth commutator but does not bound it a priori

R29 synchronizes the two sharp high-pass pressure-work representations modulo a low-frequency compensator. R32 improves that compensator to an energy–enstrophy estimate, but RD015 warns that the sharp spherical cutoff cannot be silently treated as a uniformly bounded non-`L^2` smooth multiplier.

R33 rebuilds the synchronization identity with a **smooth square partition** that preserves exactness and converts the representation defect into a genuine commutator.

Let

`L=omega×u`, `G=|u|u`.

Choose real, even Fourier multipliers `M_K,H_K` that are self-adjoint, commute with `P,Q`, and satisfy on every Fourier mode

> `M_K^2 + H_K^2 = I`.

A concrete smooth construction is to choose a smooth radial angle `theta_K(k)` with `theta_K=0` on low frequencies and `theta_K=pi/2` on sufficiently high frequencies, and set

`m_K(k)=cos(theta_K(k))`,

`h_K(k)=sin(theta_K(k))`.

Then `m_K^2+h_K^2=1` exactly. No sharp spherical multiplier estimate is used.

## 1. Exact smooth decomposition of the gradient pressure work

R03 gives

`W_3=<QL,QG>`.

Since `M_K,H_K` are self-adjoint and commute with `Q`,

`<M_K QL,M_K QG>`

`=<QL,M_K^2 QG>`,

and similarly for `H_K`. Therefore

> `W_3`
>
> `=<M_K QL,M_K QG>`
>
> ` +<H_K QL,H_K QG>`.

Define

`W_grad,M=<M_K QL,M_K QG>`,

`W_grad,H=<H_K QL,H_K QG>`.

Then exactly

> `W_3=W_grad,M+W_grad,H`.

Unlike a smooth partition satisfying only `M+H=I`, the square partition preserves the bilinear energy/work decomposition without cross terms.

## 2. Exact complementary solenoidal decomposition

R05 gives

`W_3=-<PL,PG>`.

The identical square-partition argument yields

> `W_3=W_sol,M+W_sol,H`,

where

`W_sol,M=-<M_K PL,M_K PG>`,

`W_sol,H=-<H_K PL,H_K PG>`.

Thus both exact pressure-work representations admit the same smooth low/high split.

## 3. High-filter mismatch equals minus low-filter mismatch

By P/Q orthogonality and complementarity,

`W_grad,H-W_sol,H`

`=<H_K L,H_K G>`

`=<H_K^2 L,G>`.

Pointwise Lamb/test orthogonality gives

`L·G=(omega×u)·(|u|u)=0`,

hence

`<L,G>=0`.

Using `H_K^2=I-M_K^2`,

`<H_K^2L,G>`

`=-<M_K^2L,G>`

`=-<M_KL,M_KG>`.

Therefore

> `W_grad,H-W_sol,H`
>
> `=-<M_KL,M_KG>`.

Likewise

> `W_grad,M-W_sol,M`
>
> `=<M_KL,M_KG>`.

So the low and high representation defects still cancel **exactly**, even though the smooth filters overlap in frequency.

This is the smooth analogue of R29.

## 4. The low defect is a pure commutator

Let `C_u` denote the pointwise cross-product multiplication operator

`C_u omega = omega×u`.

The low defect is

`D_M=<M_K^2(omega×u),G>`.

Add and subtract `(M_K^2 omega)×u`:

`M_K^2(omega×u)`

`=[M_K^2,C_u]omega +(M_K^2 omega)×u`.

But the second term is pointwise orthogonal to

`G=|u|u`:

`((M_K^2 omega)×u)·G=0`.

Hence exactly

> `D_M`
>
> `=< [M_K^2,C_u] omega, G>`.

Consequently the high-filter cross-representation mismatch is

> `W_grad,H-W_sol,H`
>
> `=-< [M_K^2,C_u] omega,G>`.

This is the key R33 representation: the defect is **not a generic low-frequency product**. It is entirely generated by failure of the smooth Fourier filter to commute with multiplication by the velocity inside the Lamb cross product.

## 5. Kernel form of the commutator

When `M_K^2` is realized by convolution with a smooth periodic kernel `K_K`, the commutator has the exact formal kernel structure

`[M_K^2,C_u]omega(x)`

`=int K_K(x-y)`

`  [omega(y)×(u(y)-u(x))] dy`.

This shows where any additional cancellation must come from: **velocity increments across the filter scale**.

R33 does not assign a universal norm estimate to this formula. Such an estimate must be proved in the precise periodic smooth-multiplier setting and must respect critical scaling.

## 6. Why the smooth redesign matters

RD015 prevents the proof from using a hidden `K`-uniform `L^p` bound for the sharp ball cutoff.

R33 avoids that issue at the representation level:

- exactness comes from `M_K^2+H_K^2=I`, not disjoint support;
- symbols may be chosen genuinely smooth;
- the mismatch becomes a standard commutator-shaped object;
- smooth-kernel moment estimates, paraproduct decompositions and Calderon–Zygmund commutator tools become legitimate candidates, provided their hypotheses and constants are proved.

This does not mean such tools automatically close Navier–Stokes.

## 7. Scaling design

Choose the symbol family by rescaling a fixed smooth profile,

`m_K(k)=m(k/K)`.

Under Navier–Stokes spatial rescaling the cutoff parameter is transformed together with the solution. The resulting family is therefore scale-covariant.

The commutator contains one filter-scale difference of `u`; any useful estimate must spend that gain without demanding an uncontrolled derivative or endpoint norm.

## 8. Immediate no-shortcut audit

The most naive kernel estimate uses

`u(y)-u(x) ~ (y-x) grad u`

and therefore gains one factor of `K^{-1}`. But combining only

`grad u in L^2`, `omega in L^2`

puts the commutator naturally near `L^1`, while pairing with `G=|u|u` would then demand an unavailable `L^infinity` control.

Thus R33 does **not** claim that the visible `K^{-1}` moment alone closes the defect.

The next theorem must exploit more structure — for example a better exponent allocation, the transported-speed geometry of R24, div–curl compensation, or a pressure-work-specific paraproduct cancellation.

## 9. Sharpened frontier

After R33 the harmonic-analysis target is no longer

> improve the crude low-frequency product estimate somehow.

It is the specific question

> **Can the smooth commutator pairing**
>
> ` <[M_K^2,C_u]omega, |u|u> `
>
> **be controlled at critical scale strongly enough to make the common productive UV packets of R31 impossible?**

A useful result should either

1. improve the generic R32 `~K^2` compensator scale;
2. produce a parabolic-window summable defect;
3. connect directly to `A_bal,>K` from R30; or
4. force a known endpoint regularity criterion.

R33 itself is an exact representation theorem, not that estimate.
