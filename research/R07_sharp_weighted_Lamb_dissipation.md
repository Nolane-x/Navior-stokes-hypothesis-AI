# R07 — Sharp weighted Lamb-force control by the `L^p` diffusion geometry

**Status:** `exact structural theorem / not claimed novel`  
**Depends on:** R05  
**Primary case:** `p=3`  
**Clay status:** verified partial structure only; it does not control the nonlocal Helmholtz projection in the same weight

R05 shows that the pressure obstruction in every `L^p` balance is generated by the projected Lamb force. R07 identifies an exact local fact that is hidden by an unweighted estimate: the **unprojected** Lamb force is already controlled by the same `L^p` diffusion, provided it is measured in its natural amplitude weight.

Let

`u = rho n`, `rho=|u|`, `|n|=1`

where `rho>0`, and let

`omega=curl u`, `L=omega x u`.

## 1. Exact amplitude–direction formula for the Lamb force

Using

`curl(rho n)=grad rho x n + rho curl n`

and the unit-vector identity

`(curl n) x n = (n·grad)n`

(which follows from `(n·grad)n = grad(|n|^2/2) + (curl n)x n` and `|n|=1`), we obtain

> `L = -rho grad_perp rho + rho^2 (n·grad)n`,

where

`grad_perp rho = grad rho - n(n·grad rho)`.

Thus the Lamb force sees two geometric effects:

1. transverse amplitude variation;
2. curvature/turning of the velocity direction along streamlines.

The direct longitudinal amplitude derivative `n·grad rho` is absent from `L`.

## 2. Exact `L^p` diffusion geometry

For `p>=2`, the diffusion functional in the `L^p` balance is

`D_p = ∫ [ rho^(p-2)|grad u|^2`

`          + (p-2)rho^(p-4) Σ_j (u·partial_j u)^2 ]`.

On `{rho>0}`, amplitude–direction decomposition gives

> `D_p = ∫ [ (p-1)rho^(p-2)|grad rho|^2`

> `          + rho^p |grad n|^2 ]`.

## 3. Weighted Lamb inequality

Pointwise on `{rho>0}`,

`rho^(p-4)|L|^2`

`= rho^(p-2)| -grad_perp rho + rho(n·grad)n |^2`.

For arbitrary vectors `a,b` and `p>1`,

`|a+b|^2 <= [p/(p-1)] [ (p-1)|a|^2 + |b|^2 ]`.

This constant is sharp for the underlying two-vector quadratic inequality, since

`sup_(x,y) (x+y)^2 / ((p-1)x^2+y^2) = 1/(p-1)+1 = p/(p-1)`.

Using

`|grad_perp rho| <= |grad rho|`,

`|(n·grad)n| <= |grad n|`,

we obtain

> `rho^(p-4)|omega x u|^2`

> `<= [p/(p-1)] [ (p-1)rho^(p-2)|grad rho|^2 + rho^p|grad n|^2 ]`.

After integration,

> `∫ rho^(p-4)|omega x u|^2 <= [p/(p-1)] D_p`.

For `p>2`, the integrand on the zero set may be defined by the natural limiting/regularized value; the primary `p=3` case is non-singular after the factor `|L|^2=O(rho^2)` is taken into account. The theorem may equivalently be stated first on nonvanishing smooth fields and then extended by standard regularization.

## 4. Critical `p=3` corollary

At the critical velocity exponent,

> `∫ |omega x u|^2 / |u| <= (3/2) D_3`.

This is the key R07 statement.

Together with the exact Lamb decomposition, it says that the R01 diffusion already controls the full local Lamb force after division by one power of amplitude.

## 5. Why this does not solve R06

R06 needs the unweighted projected norm

`||Q(omega x u)||_2^2`.

R07 controls instead the weighted full norm

`∫ |omega x u|^2 / |u|`.

The Helmholtz projector is nonlocal and does not commute with multiplication by `|u|^(±1/2)`. Therefore one cannot pass from R07 to

`∫ |Q(omega x u)|^2 / |u| <= C D_3`

with a universal constant without a weighted singular-integral theorem whose constant depends on the geometry of the weight `|u|`.

This identifies the remaining obstruction more sharply:

> the local nonlinear geometry is already diffusion-controlled; the hard step is transporting that control through a nonlocal Helmholtz projection across a dynamically generated, possibly degenerate amplitude weight.

## 6. Muckenhoupt/weighted-projection research fork

R07 suggests a concrete conditional route. If the weights generated by the amplitude satisfy a quantitative condition under which the Helmholtz/Riesz projectors are bounded in the needed weighted `L^2` spaces with controlled constants, then the R07 local estimate could be transferred to the Bernoulli/Lamb defect of R06.

This is **not** asserted here: velocity amplitudes can vanish, and no global `A_2`-type bound for `|u|` or `1/|u|` is known or assumed.

The next proof obligation is therefore adversarial:

1. determine the exact weighted projector estimate that would be sufficient;
2. test whether smooth divergence-free fields can make the relevant weight characteristic arbitrarily bad;
3. if statewise control fails, ask whether actual Navier–Stokes evolution enforces a weaker time-integrated weighted bound.

## 7. Relation to prior no-gos

- RD001 killed unweighted constant absorption `W_3<=C D_3`.
- RD002 killed a candidate static projected-angle half-gap.
- RD003 killed generic fixed quadratic convective Lyapunov damping.

R07 survives all three because it is a **local weighted identity/inequality** rather than a global statewise pressure absorption theorem. Its failure to close is now localized to the nonlocal weighted Helmholtz step rather than to the pointwise Lamb geometry itself.
