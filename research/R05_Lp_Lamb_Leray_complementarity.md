# R05 — `L^p` Lamb–Leray complementarity family

**Status:** `exact structural theorem / classical identities recombined; not claimed novel`  
**Depends on:** R01–R04  
**Clay status:** verified partial structure only; not global regularity

R03/R04 isolate the critical `p=3` pressure obstruction. R05 shows that the same geometry is part of an exact continuum of `L^p` identities whose protected endpoint is kinetic energy (`p=2`). This exposes what changes between energy control and the critical `L^3` barrier.

Let `u` be a smooth periodic divergence-free solution and let `p>=2`. Set

`rho=|u|`,

`G_p = rho^(p-2) u`,

`L = omega x u`, `omega=curl u`,

and let `P` be the periodic Leray projector and `Q=I-P`.

## 1. Exact `L^p` balance

Testing Navier–Stokes against `G_p` gives

`(1/p) d/dt ∫rho^p + nu D_p(u) = W_p(u,pres)`,

where

`D_p(u) = ∫ [ rho^(p-2)|grad u|^2`

`             + (p-2)rho^(p-4) Σ_j (u·partial_j u)^2 ]`

(with the usual continuous/regularized interpretation at `rho=0`) and

`W_p = -<grad pres, G_p>`.

Equivalently,

`W_p = (p-2) ∫ pres rho^(p-3) u·grad rho`.

The convection term cancels exactly because

`(u·grad)u · G_p = (1/p)u·grad(rho^p)`.

## 2. Lamb factorization for every `p>=2`

The Lamb identity gives

`(u·grad)u = L + grad(rho^2/2)`.

The kinetic gradient is invisible to `G_p`:

`<grad(rho^2/2), G_p>`

`= ∫ rho^(p-1) u·grad rho`

`= (1/p)∫u·grad(rho^p) = 0`.

Since `QN=-grad pres`, it follows that

> `W_p = < QL, QG_p >`.

This is the all-`p` analogue of R03.

## 3. Exact complementary solenoidal representation

Pointwise,

`L·G_p = (omega x u)·(rho^(p-2)u)=0`.

Orthogonal Helmholtz decomposition therefore yields

`<PL,PG_p> + <QL,QG_p> = 0`, hence

> `W_p = - < PL, PG_p >`.

But in Lamb form the projected Navier–Stokes equation is

`partial_t u + PL = nu Delta u`.

Thus the same nonlinear force `PL` that drives the actual solenoidal velocity dynamics is exactly the complementary partner of the pressure work in every `L^p` balance.

This is stronger conceptually than treating pressure as an external nuisance: pressure growth and projected nonlinear acceleration are two complementary Helmholtz views of the same Lamb interaction.

## 4. Universal orthogonal half-bound for the entire family

Applying the R04 projector lemma to the orthogonal pair `(L,G_p)` gives

> `|W_p| <= (1/2) ||omega x u||_2 ||u||_(2p-2)^(p-1)`.

The constant `1/2` is sharp at the abstract Hilbert-space level. Additional improvement would need PDE/vector-field structure beyond mere pointwise orthogonality.

## 5. Why `p=2` and `p=3` are qualitatively different

At `p=2`,

`G_2=u` is divergence-free, so `QG_2=0` and therefore

`W_2=0`.

This recovers the exact kinetic-energy cancellation.

At `p=3`,

`G_3=|u|u`

is no longer divergence-free, and R01–R04 show that its Helmholtz defect couples to the Lamb defect. `L^3` is also the scale-invariant velocity norm in three dimensions.

Therefore the unresolved transition can be phrased precisely:

> energy is protected because the nonlinear test field stays entirely in the solenoidal subspace at `p=2`; the critical `p=3` obstruction is generated by the Helmholtz defect acquired by the nonlinear test field as one moves away from `p=2`.

## 6. Tangent-at-energy representation

Formally, for `p=2+epsilon`,

`G_p = rho^epsilon u`

`    = u + epsilon (log rho)u + O(epsilon^2)`

away from the zero set. Since `Qu=0`, the first Helmholtz defect turns on as

`QG_p = epsilon Q[(log rho)u] + O(epsilon^2)`.

This suggests a trajectory question sharper than a static angle bound: can Navier–Stokes dynamics control the growth of the nonlinear test-field defect as `p` moves from the protected energy endpoint toward the critical endpoint `p=3`?

The logarithmic expansion is only a representation heuristic near zeros unless regularized; it is **not** used as a theorem in the proof chain.

## 7. Scale audit

Under Euclidean scaling `u_lambda(x,t)=lambda u(lambda x,lambda^2 t)` in three dimensions,

- `W_p` scales as `lambda^(p-1)`;
- `||omega x u||_2` scales as `lambda^(3/2)`;
- `||u||_(2p-2)^(p-1)` scales as `lambda^(p-5/2)`;
- their product scales as `lambda^(p-1)`.

So the R05 half-bound is dimensionally exact for every `p`. Only `p=3` makes the underlying velocity `L^p` norm itself scale invariant.

## Open proof obligation created by R05

R05 does not control `||omega x u||_2`, nor does it yield a strict projected-angle gap. Its value is to identify a dynamic bridge unavailable to arbitrary statewise inequalities:

1. estimate the evolution/history of `PG_p` or `QG_p` along the PDE;
2. couple it to the actual projected Lamb force `PL` appearing in `partial_t u`;
3. obtain a scale-critical integrated depletion as `p` approaches `3`;
4. only then attempt to close the R01 critical `L^3` balance.

This trajectory-dependent program survives RD001 and RD002 because those no-gos concern arbitrary instantaneous states, not histories satisfying Navier–Stokes evolution.
