# C03 — Atomic endpoint full-tail rigidity challenger

**Status:** `independent literature challenger / not imported as a repository theorem`  
**Interfaces with:** R31, R38–R42  
**Primary source:** Hao Huang, *Full-Tail Dynamical Rigidity Forced by Atomic Navier-Stokes Energy Concentration*, arXiv:2608.04138 (2026)

## Why this challenger matters

R41–R42 extract normalized ultraviolet work bursts with vanishing duration and unweighted enstrophy cost, while forcing productive output frequency escape and spectral multiplicity explosion.  That is a Fourier/time normalization.  It does not yet select a spatial center or produce compactness after rescaling.

Huang's 2026 preprint studies a complementary spatial endpoint mechanism for a smooth unforced Navier–Stokes branch on the flat torus.  Its stated results include:

1. existence of a unique full-time endpoint kinetic-energy measure `mu_*`;
2. if `mu_*` has a point atom, then that atom forces a same-parent full-tail local-Hodge/Oseen saturation family;
3. the same atom forces failure of a parent-only delayed second-order evolution budget arbitrarily close to the endpoint.

The paper does **not** claim global regularity and does not exclude all endpoint atoms from energy bounds alone.

## Challenger question for the Nolane proof spine

R41–R42 now produce a canonical sequence of normalized terminal work bursts.  A decisive discrimination task is:

> Can an R41 unit-burst sequence be spatially localized so that a nonzero fraction of its parent kinetic energy converges to a point atom of the unique endpoint measure?

If yes, the atomic branch should be confronted with the independent full-tail rigidity/operator obstruction of arXiv:2608.04138 rather than building another Fourier-only synchronization theorem.

If no, then any hypothetical singular mechanism compatible with R41–R42 must live in a **non-atomic/diffuse endpoint-energy branch**, and the proof program should seek a spatial nonconcentration theorem or a critical rescaling compactness mechanism for that branch.

## Why this is not a proof step yet

The current repository has not independently reconstructed the full Oseen-adjoint/atomic-rigidity argument of the preprint.  More importantly, R41's unit common-work normalization does not itself imply that the kinetic-energy endpoint measure has an atom.

Therefore C03 is used only as an independent challenger and route selector:

- **atomic branch:** compare against same-parent full-tail Oseen rigidity;
- **non-atomic branch:** develop diffuse spatial/critical compactness;
- **do not** infer global regularity from the dichotomy alone.

## Interface with R42 multiplicity

R42 proves that one normalized burst requires an exploding number of productive Fourier output modes.  Fourier multiplicity does not decide spatial atomicity: a highly multimode field may still concentrate spatially, while a spectrally broad field may remain diffuse.

Thus C03 supplies a genuinely different representation from the helical/Fourier proof spine and is suitable as a W5 independent challenger rather than a duplicate theorem.
