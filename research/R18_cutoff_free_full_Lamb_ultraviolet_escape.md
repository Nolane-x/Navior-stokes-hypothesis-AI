# R18 — Cutoff-free full-Lamb ultraviolet escape

**Status:** `exact structural sharpening / correction of the R13 physical interpretation; not claimed novel`  
**Depends on:** R08, R13, R14–R17, RD010  
**Clay status:** does not bound the ultraviolet action; no global regularity conclusion

R13 proves an ultraviolet escape statement for the sharp high-amplitude tail field

`F=1_{|u|>M_*}(omega×u)`.

RD010 shows that high Fourier modes of `QF` may be generated by the sharp amplitude cutoff itself, even when the physical Lamb force `L=omega×u` has only low Fourier support. Thus R13 remains mathematically valid, but its high modes should not be identified without qualification with physical high-frequency Lamb triads.

R18 removes this ambiguity completely.

Let

`U(t)=||u(t)||_{3/2}`,

`L(t)=omega(t)×u(t)`,

and define the **cutoff-free full-Lamb action**

> `A_L(T)=int_0^T U(t) ||L(t)||_2^2 dt`.

## 1. The R08 tail action is dominated by the full-Lamb action

The amplitude indicator is an `L^2` contraction and the gradient Helmholtz projector `Q` is an orthogonal `L^2` contraction. Therefore

`||Q(1_{|u|>M_*}L)||_2 <= ||L||_2`.

Hence exactly

> `A_tail(T) <= A_L(T)`.

Consequently, if a finite-time obstruction through the R08 route forces

`A_tail(T*)=infinity`,

then necessarily

> `A_L(T*)=infinity`.

This implication contains no Fourier localization and no cutoff interpretation.

## 2. Every fixed low-frequency portion of the full-Lamb action is energy controlled

Let `P_{<=K}` be the orthogonal Fourier projector onto a fixed finite set of modes `|k|<=K`. Finite-dimensional Bernstein control gives

`||P_{<=K} L||_2 <= C_B(K)||L||_1`.

By Holder,

`||L||_1 <= ||omega||_2 ||u||_2`.

On the unit periodic domain,

`U=||u||_{3/2} <= ||u||_2 <= E0`,

where `E0=||u_0||_2`, and the energy identity/inequality gives

`2 nu int_0^T ||omega||_2^2 dt <= E0^2`.

Therefore

`int_0^T U ||P_{<=K}L||_2^2 dt`

`<= C_B(K)^2 E0^3 int_0^T ||omega||_2^2 dt`

and hence

> `int_0^T U ||P_{<=K}L||_2^2 dt`
>
> `<= [C_B(K)^2/(2 nu)] E0^5 < infinity`.

This is the same finite-frequency energy mechanism as R13, now applied to the untruncated physical Lamb force.

## 3. Genuine physical ultraviolet escape

Because `P_{<=K}` and `P_{>K}` are orthogonal,

`||L||_2^2 = ||P_{<=K}L||_2^2 + ||P_{>K}L||_2^2`.

If `A_tail(T*)=infinity`, then Section 1 gives `A_L(T*)=infinity`. Section 2 says the low-frequency full-Lamb action is finite for every fixed `K`. Therefore, for every fixed finite `K`,

> `int_0^{T*} U(t)||P_{>K}L(t)||_2^2 dt = infinity`.

This is a cutoff-free ultraviolet escape theorem for the **actual physical Lamb force**.

The statement is stronger semantically than the R13 interpretation, not stronger as a global regularity estimate: it still supplies no summable control as `K->infinity`.

## 4. Critical scaling

Under the Euclidean Navier–Stokes scaling

`u_lambda(x,t)=lambda u(lambda x,lambda^2 t)`,

`U=||u||_{3/2}` has exponent `-1`,

`||L||_2^2` has exponent `+3`,

and `dt` has exponent `-2`.

Thus

> `A_L` is scale invariant.

The corrected physical ultraviolet statement therefore remains located at the critical scaling of the Millennium problem.

## 5. Clean bridge to the helical program

Unlike the sharp-truncated field in R13, the full Lamb force is exactly the object factorized in R14/R15:

`L=2 lambda_* u_+×u_- + r_*×u`.

R16 then gives

`A_L <= 8 A_spin + 2 A_bw`

by the same pointwise square splitting used there. Combining with R18 yields the clean implication

> `A_tail(T*)=infinity`
>
> `=> A_L(T*)=infinity`
>
> `=> A_spin(T*)=infinity or A_bw(T*)=infinity (or both)`,

while R18 additionally guarantees that the diverging full-Lamb action escapes every fixed physical Fourier cutoff.

This removes the sharp-cutoff ambiguity from the R14–R17 helical/bandwidth frontier.

## 6. Corrected research frontier

A positive proof through this route must now control a genuinely physical critical ultraviolet action. The strongest next targets are:

1. derive frequency-local helical triad identities directly for `P_j(omega×u)`;
2. control the centered bandwidth-production determinant from R17 over parabolic scale-time boxes;
3. prove a scale-by-scale interaction law coupling `A_spin` and `A_bw` so that their physical UV tails cannot both remain non-summable;
4. construct a minimal rescaled object carrying divergent full-Lamb action and derive a rigidity/Liouville contradiction.

R18 does not prove any of these closing statements.

## 7. Scope correction to R13

R13 itself is retained unchanged as an exact theorem about the truncated field `QF`. RD010 and R18 refine only the interpretation and downstream representation:

- **R13:** truncated-tail action must escape to high Fourier modes;
- **RD010:** those modes may partly be cutoff-generated;
- **R18:** if the R08 tail action diverges, the untruncated physical Lamb force also has divergent scale-critical action escaping every fixed Fourier cutoff.

This is the canonical physical-UV statement from this point forward.
