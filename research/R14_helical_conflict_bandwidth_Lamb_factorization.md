# R14 — Helical conflict + radial-bandwidth factorization of the Lamb force

**Status:** `exact structural factorization / not claimed novel`  
**Depends on:** R03, R13  
**Literature baseline:** curl/spin decomposition and blow-up spin conflict are studied by Lerner–Vigneron (arXiv:2203.07950)  
**Clay status:** no global estimate is proved

R13 shows that a divergent R08 tail action must escape to arbitrarily high Fourier frequencies, while RD008 shows that energy alone cannot make that ultraviolet cascade summable. R14 injects structure specific to the true three-dimensional curl/Lamb nonlinearity.

Let

`D = sqrt(-Delta)`

on mean-zero divergence-free vector fields, and define the helical/spin projections

`u_+ = (1/2)(u + D^(-1) curl u)`,

`u_- = (1/2)(u - D^(-1) curl u)`.

Then

`u=u_+ + u_-`,

`curl u_+ = +D u_+`,

`curl u_- = -D u_-`,

so

`omega=curl u = D u_+ - D u_-`.

## 1. Exact reference-frequency factorization

Fix any reference frequency `lambda>0` and define the radial-dispersion remainder

`r_lambda = (D-lambda)u_+ - (D-lambda)u_-`.

Then

`omega = lambda(u_+-u_-) + r_lambda`.

Crossing with `u=u_++u_-` gives

`omega×u`

`= lambda(u_+-u_-)×(u_++u_-) + r_lambda×u`

and the self-cross terms vanish. Therefore

> `omega×u = 2 lambda u_+×u_- + r_lambda×u`.

This identity is exact for every `lambda`.

## 2. Meaning of the two terms

The Lamb force is generated by two distinct mechanisms:

1. **helical/spin conflict**: `2 lambda u_+×u_-`;
2. **radial spectral dispersion**: `r_lambda×u`, measuring the failure of each spin sector to sit on the reference sphere `|k|=lambda`.

A field that is simultaneously

- single-spin (`u_-=0` or `u_+=0`), and
- exactly monochromatic (`D u=lambda u`)

has

`omega×u=0`.

This is the Beltrami endpoint of the factorization.

## 3. Norm consequences

By Holder,

> `||omega×u||_1`

> `<= 2 lambda ||u_+||_2 ||u_-||_2`

> `   + ||r_lambda||_2 ||u||_2`.

Similarly,

> `||omega×u||_2`

> `<= 2 lambda ||u_+||_4 ||u_-||_4`

> `   + ||r_lambda||_4 ||u||_4`.

Thus a large Lamb force requires either substantial coexistence of the two spin sectors or substantial radial bandwidth (in the corresponding norms), or both.

## 4. Dimensionless conflict/dispersion parameters

When `u!=0`, define the energy-level spin-mixing factor

`m_spin = 2 ||u_+||_2 ||u_-||_2 / ||u||_2^2`,

so `0<=m_spin<=1`, and

`delta_lambda = ||r_lambda||_2 / ||u||_2`.

Then

> `||omega×u||_1 <= [lambda m_spin + delta_lambda] ||u||_2^2`.

Optimizing over `lambda` turns the right side into a quantitative measure of the distance from a narrow-band single-spin/Beltrami configuration.

## 5. Connection to R13 finite-frequency control

For the R08 truncated Lamb field

`F=1_{|u|>M_*}(omega×u)`,

we still have `||F||_1<=||omega×u||_1`. Therefore R13 sharpens to

`||P_{<=K}QF||_2`

`<= C_B(K)[2 lambda ||u_+||_2||u_-||_2 + ||r_lambda||_2||u||_2]`.

This does not solve the ultraviolet problem because the finite-frequency Bernstein constant grows with `K`, but it replaces a generic energy bound by a true-NS geometric quantity.

## 6. Literature consistency

Lerner and Vigneron diagonalize curl into two spin-definite components and report that, in a hypothetical finite-time Navier–Stokes blow-up, both spin components must blow up simultaneously and with equal rates. That established result means a general proof cannot simply assume that one helical sector becomes negligible near singularity.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.07950

R14 is consistent with that picture: a singular ultraviolet cascade may require a persistent conflict between the two spin sectors, while broadband dispersion supplies a second possible Lamb mechanism.

## 7. New frontier

The H3 route is now sharpened to a dichotomy:

> a non-summable R13 ultraviolet tail cascade must be sustained through high-frequency spin conflict, radial spectral dispersion, or both.

Potential proof targets are:

1. derive a critical spacetime bound on the cross-spin product localized to the R08 high-amplitude tail;
2. show that persistent radial dispersion incurs a summable dissipation cost;
3. use helicity balance to constrain the simultaneous growth of both spin sectors across successive ultraviolet scales;
4. prove that a minimal critical tail bubble cannot maintain both spin conflict and radial bandwidth under rescaling.

No such closing estimate is proved in R14.
